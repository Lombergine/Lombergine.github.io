The International Canaries

2026

Independent replication attempt

Stanford's Digital Economy Lab found a 19% employment shortfall for 22 to 25 year olds in AI-exposed occupations, using US payroll microdata. I tried to find the same signal across 26 countries using only public World Bank and ILO data.

It does not replicate, and the reason is the interesting part. The correlation is +0.28 (p = 0.16) and the sign flips to −0.02 when Greece, Spain and Italy are dropped. The apparent signal is post-COVID recovery, not AI. The placebo on the pre-ChatGPT window is clean at +0.03, so there is no pre-trend, just no power at this resolution.

Annual country-level estimates average away exactly the variation the effect lives in. The version worth building is EU-LFS occupation-by-age counts crossed with ISCO-mapped exposure scores.
